Water is spreading through your San Gabriel home

Shut the source first. That's the valve at the fixture, or the main if the fixture is unclear. Then lift anything porous off the floor, because carpet, paper and cardboard soak up water in minutes.

Turn off power in the wet area only when you can reach the breaker without standing in water. If water is reaching outlets or the panel, don't go near it. Call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas risk, then call us.

A clean supply line is Category 1 under ANSI/IICRC S500, but it can become Category 2 or 3 as it sits and touches soiled materials. Sewage is Category 3 from the start. Crews use portable or truck-mounted extraction, then moisture meters and thermal imaging to map what's wet, and LGR dehumidifiers with air movers to pull the rest out.

Repair and cleanup, the two jobs San Gabriel callers ask about

Water damage repair is the rebuilding side: replacement drywall, new baseboards, flooring and paint, after the structure has been dried and read as dry. It doesn't fix the leaking plumbing itself, and we'll tell you if a plumber needs to come first.

Water damage cleanup covers extraction, removal of materials that can't be saved, and cleaning of surfaces that can. On many jobs the two overlap, and we put the scope on paper before any work begins, so you know where one ends and the other begins.

If the first hours were missed and surfaces have started to turn, our mold remediation page explains how that process follows the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard. For anything about your health, ask a physician.

Golden State Water serves portions of San Gabriel through its San Gabriel service area, not every address. Your bill shows who supplies your water. If the break is on the supplier's side of the meter, they need the call, and if it's after the meter, it's yours and ours.

Before we arrive: what to do while water is still spreading

Find the shutoff

Close the valve under the sink, behind the toilet or at the washer. If you can't find it, shut the main.

Kill power to wet rooms, safely

Use the breaker only when the panel is dry and clear. Water near outlets means stay back.

Move the keepsakes

Photos, documents, laptops and shoes go up high first, then rugs and chair legs.

Stay out of dirty water

Sewage or a backed-up drain is contaminated, so keep kids and pets away from it.

Phone the dispatcher
