What drives the cost of water damage work in El Monte

We don't post prices because no two houses are alike. Cost follows five things: how much water, how long it sat, which materials got wet, how much of the building is affected and what has to be rebuilt afterward. A short, clean supply-line leak dried the same day is a very different job from sewage that sat in carpet.

The LA County consumer office lists what a written contract should state: total price and payment schedule, scope and change-order terms, timeline and permits, the contractor's license number and the cancellation policy. Ask for those in writing, and treat a demand for most of the money upfront as a warning sign. California caps down payments, and the county publishes the limit.

Verify any contractor's license at cslb.ca.gov, ask for proof of liability insurance and use lien waivers with each payment. We're licensed and insured, and we explain scope and price before any work starts.

Insurance basics: sudden, gradual and flood

Sudden and accidental water damage, such as a burst pipe, is often covered by a homeowners policy. Gradual leaks and neglect often are not. Check your own policy, then ask your insurer what applies to your loss.

Flood is different. The California Department of Insurance says standard homeowners' insurance does not cover flood damage, and flood protection is sold under the federal NFIP and usually begin 30 days after purchase. Photos, moisture readings and a written scope all help document a claim, and we provide them. We can't promise a claim outcome.

Water spreading across an El Monte floor

Stop the source before anything else. Close the fixture valve or the main, then lift valuables and move furniture out of the wet area. If the water came up through a drain or in from outside, treat it as grossly contaminated, which is Category 3 under ANSI/IICRC S500.

Kill power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel dry-footed. If water is touching outlets or the panel, stay out and ask the utility or 911 about the hazard before you phone us. Emergency water removal then starts with pumps and extractors, followed by moisture meters, air movers and LGR dehumidifiers.

Before we arrive: what to do while water is still spreading

Close the main if unsure

If you can't see what is leaking, shut the main valve and stop the supply.

Be careful with power

Turn off wet circuits only when the panel is dry. Water at outlets means call the utility first.

Get things above the water

Furniture legs, bottom shelves and floor boxes go up first, and clothing and papers follow.

Skip the standing water

Floodwater and sewage are grossly contaminated, so keep children and pets away.
