Why it matters

Why the first two days decide the outcome

Emergency water damage gets worse by the hour. Drywall wicks water upward, carpet pad holds moisture against the subfloor, and the EPA notes that drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours usually prevents mold growth. That window is why the emergency line is staffed 24/7 instead of during office hours.

Restoration is not one task. It is extraction, drying, cleanup and repair done in a sensible order, and skipping the drying step is the most common reason a home that looks fine turns musty a few weeks later. Standards such as ANSI/IICRC S500 describe how professional water damage restoration is supposed to be done, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it.

If you are not sure which service fits, start with emergency water removal when water is standing, water mitigation when it has soaked in, and water damage repair once everything is dry.

Local knowledge

Temple City homes and water

Walter P. Temple laid out the town in 1923 on land that had been part of Rancho Santa Anita, and the city incorporated in 1960. A community platted that early has many homes that are decades old, so original supply lines, aging water heaters and older drain pipes are worth a look whenever a leak shows up near Las Tunas Drive or the original townsite grid.

Temple City sits at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains. For flood exposure at your address, check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask your city about drainage.
