Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit this site or call our line.

Who we are

Temple City Water Damage Pros operates the website templecitywaterdamagepros.com and a 24/7 phone line for water damage restoration in Temple City, California and nearby San Gabriel Valley towns. Our address is 9123 Las Tunas Dr, Temple City, CA 91780.

What we collect

This site has no forms and does not ask you to type personal details. When you call us, we may ask for your name, the address of the property and a description of the damage so that we can send help.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use call information

We use the information you give us on a call to respond to your request, to arrange service, and to improve how we answer the line. We do not use the website to collect payment details.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use basic technical data such as browser type and pages viewed to keep it working and to understand which pages are useful. Embedded Google Maps may set their own cookies under Google's policies.

Sharing

To arrange service we share the details of your request with the restoration professionals who will visit your property. We do not sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@templecitywaterdamagepros.com. You can also decline to be recorded by ending the call and writing to us instead.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@templecitywaterdamagepros.com or to (626) 879-0276.
